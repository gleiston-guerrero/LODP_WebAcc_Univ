This EPFL privacy policy (hereinafter “Privacy Policy”) aims at informing you how the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (hereinafter “EPFL” or “We” or “Us”) may process your personal data and the rights to which you are entitled. This Privacy Policy provide general information applicable in most situations and may be supplemented with more specific notices or regulations whenever applicable. Last updated: March 25th, 2022. We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted to this page. Definition list Personal data any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, according to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (hereinafter “FADP”). Additional information Who is responsible for the processing of your personal data Information about the processing of Personal Data. Personal data we collect about you and for which purpose EPFL processes Personal Data in particular in line with its three academic core tasks which are Education, Research and Innovation, and its supporting services. Key principles that guide us EPFL is bound to respect the principles related to data protection Your rights Rights to receive a copy of your Personal Data, request a correction, etc. Personal data security Security and data retention. International transfers Data transfer outside Switzerland in compliance with the applicable laws. With whom we share your Personal Data Central services or EPFL schools, third parties and authorities, streaming services, social media, etc. Cookies Policy Session cookies and persistent cookies: which cookies are generated by EPFL and how to control them.
