For Washington For the world

Founded in 1861, the University of Washington is one of the world’s leading public research universities. The UW is where passion meets possibility. We inspire our students to make a difference — and prepare them to take action.

Explore how the Strategic Plan will shape the UW’s future

Get to know the University of Washington’s strategic plan — a roadmap for our future. Developed with input from faculty, students, staff, alumni and community partners, the plan will guide the UW through 2030 and beyond. Grounded in the University’s values and strengths, it’ll help us meet new challenges, serve our communities and take advantage of new opportunities.
