KU Leuven Libraries and Google digitise more than 100,000 books

Over the course of three years, KU Leuven Libraries and Google have digitised 101,807 books from the KU Leuven collections. The books can now be consulted from anywhere in the world, via the KU Leuven Libraries catalogue and via Google Books. Today, this substantial project was concluded with the return of the last 5,000 books to KU Leuven from the Google scanning centre.

600 years of KU Leuven

In 2025, we celebrated six centuries of wonder and curiosity. The year was packed with activities for young and old alike and kicked off with our festive weekend “KU Leuven Inside”. We organised an art and science trail, held a large-scale cantus for alumni and presented fascinating exhibitions on the past and present of our university. 

We continue to write our history. Ready for a new century full of questions and answers, because knowledge knows no end.
