    KU Leuven processes your personal data: privacy notice

    KU Leuven is committed to high ethical standards for the processing of personal data in academic and other contexts. It adheres to the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws.

    The way in which KU Leuven processes your personal data is explained in the privacy notice as explained in the blocks below.
